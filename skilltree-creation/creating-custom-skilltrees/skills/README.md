# Skills

Skills are the abilities that Pets can gain as they level up.

| Skill                     | Description                                                               |
| ------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| [Backpack](backpack.md)   | Add a backpack (inventory) to your Pet.                                   |
| [Beacon](beacon.md)       | Turn your Pet into a walking beacon.                                      |
| [Behavior](behavior.md)   | Change the behavior of your Pet.                                          |
| [Control](control.md)     | Increase the damage your Pet deals in attacks.                            |
| [Damage](damage.md)       | Increase the damage your Pet deals in attacks.                            |
| [Fire](fire.md)           | Make your Pet's attacks potentially set fire to their opponent.           |
| [Heal](heal.md)           | Allow your Pet to self-heal.                                              |
| [Knockback](knockback.md) | Make your Pet's attacks potentially knock back their opponent.            |
| [Life](life.md)           | Increase the HP your Pet has.                                             |
| [Lightning](lightning.md) | Make your Pet's attacks potentially strike lightning upon their opponent. |
| [Pickup](pickup.md)       | Allow your Pet to pick up dropped items.                                  |
| [Poison](poison.md)       | Make your Pet's attacks potentially poison their opponent.                |
| [Ranged](ranged.md)       | Arm your Pet with the ability to shoot projectiles at an opponent.        |
| [Ride](ride.md)           | Train your Pet to be ridden.                                              |
| [Shield](shield.md)       | Train your Pet to shield you from your opponent's attacks.                |
| [Slow](slow.md)           | Make your Pet's attacks potentially slow down their opponent.             |
| [Sprint](sprint.md)       | Make your Pet sprint towards its next target.                             |
| [Stomp](stomp.md)         | Train your Pet to stomp to attack nearby targets,.                        |
| [Thorns](thorns.md)       | Make your Pet's attacks potentially reflect back against their opponent.  |
| [Wither](wither.md)       | Make your Pet's attacks potentially wither their opponent.                |
